### What I learned
- `grep` searches through text and prints the line that matches a pattern.
- `grep` searches text for patterns. `-i` ignores case. `-n` shows line numbers. `-v` inverts the match (shows lines that don't match). `-c` counts matches instead of printing lines. `-r` searches recursively through directories.
- When searching system directories, redirect permission errors with `2>/dev/null`.
